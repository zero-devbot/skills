Why Resin Wicker Is the Best Material for Outdoor Furniture

Traditional wicker is woven from natural rattan, which dries out, cracks and grows mildew when it is left outdoors. Modern outdoor wicker furniture solves this by weaving high-density polyethylene (HDPE) resin fiber — a UV-stabilized synthetic that looks and feels like rattan but is engineered to survive weather — over a powder-coated aluminum frame that cannot rust.

Every piece Veranda Wicker Co. sells meets three standards:

  • HDPE resin wicker, never PVC. HDPE fiber keeps its color and flexibility for 10+ years outdoors; cheaper PVC wicker turns brittle and fades within two to three seasons.
  • Powder-coated aluminum frames. Aluminum is light enough to rearrange and, unlike steel, cannot rust — even in coastal salt air.
  • Solution-dyed olefin cushion fabric. The color is locked into the fiber itself, so cushions resist fading, mildew and stains, and dry within hours of a rainstorm.

Common Questions About Outdoor Wicker Furniture

Quick answers to the questions we hear most. See the full FAQ page for a dozen more.

Can wicker furniture stay outside all year?

Yes — if it is synthetic resin wicker. HDPE resin wicker on an aluminum frame can stay outdoors year-round in rain, sun and snow. Natural rattan wicker cannot; it should only be used on covered porches or indoors. In harsh winters, covering resin wicker or storing cushions indoors will extend cushion life, but the furniture itself does not need to come in.

What is the difference between wicker and rattan?

Rattan is a material — a climbing palm whose vines are used to build furniture. Wicker is a weaving technique, not a material. "Wicker furniture" can be woven from rattan, bamboo, or synthetic resin. All outdoor wicker sold by Veranda Wicker Co. is woven from all-weather HDPE resin.

How long does resin wicker furniture last?

Quality HDPE resin wicker over an aluminum frame typically lasts 10–15 years outdoors with basic care. That is why we back every collection with a 5-year outdoor-use warranty — roughly double the 1–3 years typical for budget PVC wicker sets.
