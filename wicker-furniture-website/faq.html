Outdoor Wicker Furniture: Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers from our product team. If your question isn't here, ask a specialist — we answer care and buying questions for free, whether or not you're a customer.

Weather & Durability

Can outdoor wicker furniture be left in the rain?

Yes. HDPE resin wicker and powder-coated aluminum frames are unaffected by rain. Quick-dry olefin cushions can also stay out and dry within hours, though bringing cushions in during long wet spells keeps them fresher. Natural rattan wicker, by contrast, should never be left in the rain.

Can wicker furniture stay outside in winter?

Resin wicker on an aluminum frame can stay outside all winter, including snow and freeze-thaw cycles. Store cushions indoors, protect glass table tops from snow load, and optionally use a breathable cover to make spring cleanup easier. Full details in the care guide.

How long does outdoor wicker furniture last?

Quality HDPE resin wicker over an aluminum frame lasts 10–15 years outdoors with basic care. Budget PVC wicker typically lasts 2–3 years before fading and cracking. Cushions are usually the first part to wear, which is why replaceable covers matter.

Does resin wicker fade in the sun?

Solution-dyed HDPE wicker resists fading because the color runs through the entire fiber and UV inhibitors are added during extrusion — noticeable fading typically takes 8+ years of full sun. Surface-coated PVC wicker fades visibly within 1–2 seasons. Our buying guide shows how to tell the two apart.

Can outdoor wicker be used by a pool or at the coast?

Yes. HDPE fiber is unaffected by chlorinated splash and salt air, and aluminum frames cannot rust — resin wicker is one of the best materials for poolside and coastal homes. In coastal settings, rinse monthly with fresh water to keep salt film from dulling the finish.

What weight can wicker patio chairs support?

Veranda seating is tested to 350 lbs per seat (300 lbs for the hanging egg chair, limited by its stand). Reputable manufacturers publish per-seat ratings; if a listing has none, assume a light-duty frame.

Materials & Buying

What is the difference between wicker and rattan?

Wicker is a weaving technique; rattan is a material. Wicker furniture can be woven from natural rattan, bamboo, willow, or synthetic resin fiber. For outdoor use, choose wicker woven from HDPE resin — natural rattan is an indoor material.

How much does good outdoor wicker furniture cost?

Expect roughly $1,000–$2,500 for a quality HDPE 4–5-piece seating set, $900–$1,600 for a 6-seat dining set, and $350–$600 for individual lounge chairs. Sets far below those ranges are almost always PVC weave or steel frames, which cuts lifespan to a few seasons.

Is wicker furniture pet-friendly?

Resin wicker stands up well to pets: fur brushes out of the weave and the fiber resists moisture and odors. Cat claws can snag strands, but snags are a ten-minute repair (see the care guide). Olefin cushion covers unzip and machine-wash, which helps with hair and muddy paws.

Cleaning & Maintenance

How do you clean outdoor wicker furniture?

Vacuum the weave with a brush attachment, wash with warm water and mild dish soap using a soft brush, rinse with a garden hose, and air-dry. Never use a pressure washer, undiluted bleach or solvent cleaners on resin wicker. For mildew, use 1 part white vinegar to 4 parts water. Step-by-step instructions are in the care guide.
