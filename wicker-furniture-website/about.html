Outdoor Wicker Is All We Do

Veranda Wicker Co. has designed and sold all-weather wicker furniture from Wilmington, North Carolina since 2012 — one product category, done properly.

Our Story

We started after a familiar disappointment: a "premium" wicker set that cracked and faded in eighteen months on a coastal porch. Digging into why, we learned the difference wasn't craftsmanship — it was materials. The set was PVC weave on a steel frame, sold with photos indistinguishable from furniture that costs three times as much and lasts ten times as long.

So we set a simple rule that still defines the catalog: every piece we sell is HDPE resin weave on a powder-coated aluminum frame with solution-dyed cushions. No budget line, no "good-better-best" laddering where the "good" tier is designed to fail. If it's on our site, it passes the same spec.

The Wilmington Test Deck

Our office sits eight miles from the Atlantic, which turns out to be an excellent furniture laboratory: salt air, 90%-humidity summers, hurricanes, and the occasional ice storm. Every new collection spends a full year uncovered on our rooftop test deck before it goes on sale. We publish what we find — fade measurements, frame checks, cushion dry times — in each collection's listing.

The 5-Year Warranty, in Plain English

  • Covered for 5 years: frame welds and structure, weave integrity (cracking, unraveling, brittleness), and cushion fabric against fading beyond a measured standard and seam failure — all under normal, uncovered, outdoor residential use.
  • Covered forever: availability of replacement parts — feet, glass tops, cushion covers, and color-matched repair strands (repair strands are free; we mail them on request).
  • Not covered: commercial use, damage from solvents or pressure washers, wind damage to unsecured items, and normal cushion-fill softening.
  • How claims work: email photos to warranty@verandawicker.com. We answer within two business days and ship parts or replacements — we don't ask you to freight furniture back.

Who You're Talking To

We're a team of eleven: two furniture designers, a materials engineer who spent nine years in polymer extrusion, four customer specialists who have all done a rotation in the repair shop, and the warehouse crew who weather-test everything by sitting on it at lunch. When you call or write, you get one of us — there is no outsourced support tier.
