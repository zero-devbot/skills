The craft

Why handwoven beats factory-moulded

Most wicker couches sold in South Africa are imported and machine-wrapped. Ours are woven strand by strand in Johannesburg — which is why we can repair, re-weave and custom-size every piece we sell.

  • UV-stabilised weave — synthetic ranges are rated for year-round highveld sun and coastal salt air.
  • Powder-coated aluminium or hardwood frames — no rust, no borer, 3-year warranty.
  • Custom everything — sizes, weave colour, cushion fabric, and fitted waterproof covers made to measure.
  • Repairable for life — our workshop re-weaves and re-cushions, so a couch never becomes landfill.
  • Nationwide delivery — door-to-door anywhere in South Africa, free in Gauteng over R10,000.

Questions

Rattan couch questions, answered straight

How much does a handwoven rattan couch cost in South Africa?

Our couches range from R6,999 for the Marula Loveseat to R32,999 for the eight-piece Lowveld Modular Set. A standard three-seater is R12,999, cushions included.

Do you deliver nationwide?

Yes — door-to-door anywhere in South Africa. Gauteng delivery is free on orders over R10,000; elsewhere is quoted per order and typically takes 5–10 working days.

Can wicker couches stay outside in the rain?

Synthetic-weave ranges are rainproof and UV-stabilised and can live outdoors year-round. Natural rattan should stay under cover — and we make fitted waterproof covers for every couch we sell.

Are the couches made locally?

Every couch is woven by hand in our Johannesburg workshop, which is why sizes, weave colours and cushion fabrics can all be customised.

Do you supply lodges and bulk trade orders?

Yes — game lodges, guesthouses, restaurants and developers, with trade pricing from six pieces and commercial-grade fabrics.

What warranty do you offer?

Frames and weave carry a 3-year warranty; cushions carry 1 year. Repairs and re-weaving are done in our own workshop.
