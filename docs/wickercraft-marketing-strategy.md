# Wickercraft Website — Full Digital Marketing Strategy

A complete go-to-market plan for a handmade wickercraft brand (baskets, furniture, home decor, planters, lampshades, storage). Covers Google plus a custom campaign for each major social platform, with budgets, content plans, and KPIs.

---

## 1. Foundation (Do This Before Any Ads)

### Brand positioning
- **Core promise:** Handwoven, natural, durable pieces — the opposite of mass-produced plastic decor.
- **Key selling angles:** handmade / artisan story, sustainable & natural materials, "quiet luxury" home aesthetic, gift-worthiness, durability ("buy once").
- **Audience segments:**
  1. **Home decor enthusiasts** (25–45, mostly women, Pinterest/Instagram heavy) — cottagecore, boho, japandi, coastal styles.
  2. **Gift buyers** (seasonal spikes: Christmas, Mother's Day, weddings, housewarmings).
  3. **Eco-conscious shoppers** (plastic-free storage, sustainable materials).
  4. **Interior designers & boutiques** (B2B/wholesale — higher order value).

### Website conversion prep
- Product photos: natural light, lifestyle shots (in a styled room) + white-background shots (needed for Google Shopping).
- Every product page: dimensions, materials, care instructions, "handmade by…" story block, reviews widget.
- Install: **Google Analytics 4**, **Google Tag Manager**, **Meta Pixel + Conversions API**, **Pinterest Tag**, **TikTok Pixel**.
- Set up conversion events: purchase, add-to-cart, begin checkout, email signup.
- Email capture: 10% off first order popup (this list powers everything later).
- Create a **Google Merchant Center** account and product feed (title format: `Handwoven [Material] [Product] – [Size/Color]`).

## 2. Google Strategy

Google captures people **actively searching** to buy — highest intent, so it gets the biggest ad share.

### 2a. Google Business Profile (free — do first)
- Create/claim profile even if online-only ("online retailer" or workshop address).
- Post weekly (new products, behind-the-scenes), collect reviews, add product catalog.

### 2b. SEO (organic — compounding, start week 1)
- **Keyword targets:**
  - Product: "handwoven wicker basket", "rattan storage baskets", "wicker laundry basket with lid", "wicker pendant lampshade"
  - Style: "boho storage baskets", "cottagecore home decor", "natural woven planters"
  - Problem: "plastic-free storage ideas", "how to clean wicker furniture"
- **Content plan (2 blog posts/month):**
  - "Wicker vs Rattan vs Seagrass: What's the Difference?"
  - "How to Style Wicker Baskets in Every Room"
  - "How to Clean and Care for Wicker Furniture"
  - "The Story Behind Our Weaving Process"
- **Technical:** product schema markup (price, reviews, availability), fast image loading (WebP), collection pages per style ("Boho", "Coastal") and per room ("Kitchen", "Bathroom").

### 2c. Google Ads — campaign structure
| Campaign | Type | Budget share | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shopping / Performance Max | PMax with feed | 40% | Your workhorse — visual products win in Shopping |
| Brand Search | Search | 5% | Protect your brand name cheaply |
| Non-brand Search | Search | 25% | Exact/phrase: "buy wicker basket online", "handmade rattan furniture" |
| Remarketing | Display/Demand Gen | 15% | Cart abandoners + site visitors, 30-day window |
| YouTube Shorts ads | Video | 15% | Repurpose TikTok/Reels content (see YouTube section) |

- Start bidding: Maximize Conversions → switch to tROAS after ~30 conversions.
- Negative keywords: "cheap", "wholesale" (unless doing B2B), "DIY", "repair", "pattern".
- Seasonal budget boost: +50–100% in Nov–Dec and 3 weeks before Mother's Day.

## 3. Instagram — "The Lookbook"
**Role:** primary brand-building + discovery channel. Wicker is *made* for Instagram.

- **Content mix (4–5 posts/week):**
  - 40% **Reels**: weaving process time-lapses, "styling 1 basket 5 ways", before/after room styling, packing orders (ASMR).
  - 30% **carousel posts**: lifestyle photo sets, "which style is your home?" quizzes.
  - 20% **Stories daily**: polls ("which colorway next?"), restock alerts, customer photos.
  - 10% **product announcements** with Instagram Shopping tags.
- **Set up Instagram Shop** — tag every product in every post.
- **Hashtag sets** (rotate 3 sets): #wickerdecor #rattanfurniture #bohodecor #cottagecore #handmadehome #sustainablehome #basketweaving.
- **Influencer campaign:** gift product to 10–15 micro home-decor influencers (10k–80k followers) per month in exchange for Reels + tag; whitelist the best posts as ads (branded content ads).
- **Paid:** boost top-performing Reels + run Advantage+ Shopping campaigns (shared with Facebook, below).
- **KPI:** follower growth, saves (best predictor of purchase intent), Shop clicks, revenue from tagged posts.

## 4. Facebook — "The Conversion Engine"
**Role:** direct-response sales + community + gift buyers (skews slightly older = higher spend on home goods).

- **Campaigns (Meta Ads Manager, covers FB + IG placements):**
  1. **Advantage+ Shopping campaign** — catalog-based, broad targeting, 60% of Meta budget.
  2. **Retargeting** — Dynamic Product Ads to cart abandoners and product viewers (7/14/30-day windows), 25%.
  3. **Prospecting video** — weaving-process video to interests: home decor, HGTV, Anthropologie, sustainability, 15%.
- **Creative angles to test:** artisan story vs. styled room vs. unboxing vs. "plastic-free swap".
- **Organic:** 3 posts/week, join/participate in home-decor and cottagecore Facebook groups (share advice, not spam), Facebook Shop synced with Instagram.
- **Seasonal:** gift-guide carousel ads ("Gifts for the person whose home smells like cinnamon") starting Nov 1 and 3 weeks pre-Mother's Day.
- **KPI:** ROAS (target 2.5–3x by month 3), cost per purchase, repeat purchase rate.

## 5. Pinterest — "The Secret Weapon"
**Role:** the highest-intent social platform for home decor. Pinterest users **plan purchases**. For a wickercraft brand this may outperform every other social channel — treat it as a priority, not an afterthought.

- **Organic (15–25 pins/week — heavy, but pins live for months, unlike posts that die in 48h):**
  - Boards: "Wicker Storage Ideas", "Boho Living Room", "Cottagecore Kitchen", "Sustainable Home Swaps", "Basket Wall Ideas".
  - Pin formats: tall (2:3) lifestyle images with subtle text overlay ("5 Ways to Style Wicker Baskets"), Idea Pins repurposed from Reels.
  - Every pin links to a product or blog post. SEO the pin titles/descriptions like Google keywords.
- **Paid:**
  - **Shopping ads** from your catalog feed (products appear in visual search) — main spend.
  - **Keyword-targeted promoted pins** on "wicker basket", "rattan decor", "boho bedroom ideas".
  - Seasonal boards + promoted pins 6–8 weeks *before* holidays (Pinterest users plan early).
- **KPI:** outbound clicks, saves, checkout conversions (Pinterest Tag), share of traffic from Pinterest (aim: your #1 or #2 social referrer by month 4).

## 6. TikTok — "The Story & Virality Play"
**Role:** brand awareness with younger buyers + viral potential of craft content. Process videos of weaving are proven TikTok gold.

- **Content (1 video/day if possible, minimum 4/week):**
  - **Process content:** raw weaving time-lapses with trending audio, "from reed to basket in 60 seconds".
  - **ASMR:** soaking reeds, weaving sounds, packaging orders.
  - **Founder story:** "I quit my job to weave baskets" / "day in the life of a basket weaver".
  - **Trends adapted:** "things in my home that just make sense", "underrated small businesses".
  - Reply to comments with videos — huge engagement multiplier.
- **TikTok Shop:** enable if available in your country — craft products convert well with live shopping and in-video product links.
- **Paid:** Spark Ads only (boost your organic winners — never cold studio-made ads on TikTok), small budget until something proves out.
- **KPI:** views, follows, profile→site clicks; expect awareness first, revenue later. One viral process video can drive weeks of sales.

## 7. YouTube — "The Trust Builder"
**Role:** long-form depth + search traffic (YouTube is the #2 search engine) + Shorts distribution.

- **Shorts (3/week):** repost your TikToks/Reels (remove watermarks).
- **Long-form (2/month):**
  - "How It's Made: A Wicker Basket From Start to Finish" (10 min)
  - "How to Care for Wicker Furniture" (evergreen search traffic)
  - Studio/workshop tours, "small business Q&A".
- **Paid:** YouTube Shorts ads + in-stream ads using the how-it's-made video, targeted at home & garden viewers and custom-intent audiences built from your Google Search keywords (this ties into the 15% of Google Ads budget above).
- **KPI:** watch time, subscriber growth, assisted conversions in GA4.

## 8. X (Twitter) — "The Community & PR Channel"
**Role:** lowest priority for sales — use it lightly for brand personality and press.

- 3–4 posts/week: behind-the-scenes photos, small-business milestones ("order #500 shipped today"), engaging with #handmade, #shopsmall, and home-decor conversations.
- Join small-business threads and artisan communities; reply more than you post.
- Use it to reach journalists/bloggers for gift-guide inclusions (pitch home & lifestyle writers in Sept–Oct for Christmas gift guides).
- **No paid budget.** KPI: mentions, press pickups.

## 9. LinkedIn — "The Wholesale Pipeline" (optional but lucrative)
**Role:** B2B — interior designers, boutique hotels, cafes, and retail buyers. One wholesale account can equal hundreds of retail orders.

- 1–2 posts/week: founder story, sustainability practices, trade/wholesale program announcement, case study photos of commercial installs.
- Direct outreach: 10 personalized connection requests/week to interior designers and boutique buyers; offer a trade discount program and a lookbook PDF.
- **KPI:** wholesale inquiries, trade accounts opened.

## 10. Email & Retention (The Profit Multiplier)
- **Welcome flow (3 emails):** brand story + 10% code → bestsellers + styling ideas → reviews/social proof.
- **Abandoned cart flow (3 emails):** 1h reminder → 24h with product benefits → 48h with small incentive.
- **Weekly newsletter:** new products, restocks, styling tips, behind-the-scenes.
- **Post-purchase:** care instructions → review request (day 14) → cross-sell (day 30).
- Target: email = 20–25% of revenue by month 6.

## 11. Budget Allocation (example: $2,000/month ad spend — scale ratios up or down)

| Channel | Share | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Google Ads (Shopping/PMax + Search + Remarketing + YouTube) | 40% | $800 |
| Meta (Facebook + Instagram) | 30% | $600 |
| Pinterest | 20% | $400 |
| TikTok (Spark Ads) | 10% | $200 |
| X / LinkedIn | 0% | organic only |

Reallocate monthly toward whatever hits the best ROAS. In Q4, increase total budget 50–100%.

## 12. 90-Day Launch Calendar

**Month 1 — Foundation & organic**
- Week 1: pixels/GA4/Merchant Center setup, email popup + welcome flow live, claim all social handles.
- Week 2: publish 2 blog posts, build 5 Pinterest boards with 30 pins, first 5 Reels/TikToks.
- Week 3: launch Google Brand Search + Shopping/PMax, Meta retargeting (even with small traffic).
- Week 4: launch Pinterest Shopping ads; first influencer gifting batch (10 creators).

**Month 2 — Scale what works**
- Launch Meta Advantage+ prospecting and non-brand Google Search.
- Daily TikTok cadence; start YouTube Shorts.
- First long-form YouTube video ("How It's Made").
- Review data: kill worst 20% of ads, double best 20%.

**Month 3 — Optimize & expand**
- Switch Google to tROAS bidding; whitelist best influencer content as ads.
- Launch abandoned-cart and post-purchase email flows.
- Start LinkedIn wholesale outreach + press pitching for gift guides.
- Set seasonal plan for the next holiday window.

## 13. KPIs Dashboard (review weekly)

| Metric | Target by Month 3 |
|---|---|
| Blended ROAS | 2.5–3x |
| Store conversion rate | 1.5–2.5% |
| Email list growth | +500–1,000/month |
| Pinterest outbound clicks | fastest-growing traffic source |
| Instagram saves per post | trending up week-over-week |
| Repeat purchase rate | 15%+ |
| Organic (SEO) traffic | +20% month-over-month |

**The one rule:** your unfair advantage is that people love *watching things get made*. Film everything in your workshop — one process video, cut into a TikTok, a Reel, a Short, a Pinterest Idea Pin, and a YouTube long-form, is a full week of content across every platform from a single afternoon of filming.
